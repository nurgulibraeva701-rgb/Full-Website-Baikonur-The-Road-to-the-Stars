Seventy years of firsts

1955

Construction begins

Builders break ground on a new test range in the Kazakh steppe.

1957

Sputnik 1

The first satellite lifts off and the space age begins.

1961

Vostok 1

Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space.

1975

Soyuz-Apollo

Soviet and American crews dock in orbit.

1998

ISS begins

The Zarya module launches, the first piece of the International Space Station.
